## Initial concept

```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a high-fidelity visual redesign concept for a Vietnamese social deduction party web game named "MŨ TRẮNG". This is a design review image, not a screenshot of an already implemented app.
Composition: landscape design presentation with a large desktop voting screen on the left (about 70% of the canvas) and a tall mobile version on the right (about 30%). Both screens must be large, legible, fully visible, and use the same coherent system. No tilted devices, hands, or perspective distortion. The mobile design is genuinely stacked for touch, not a miniature desktop.
Visual direction: "Secret Party", playful tactile board-game / toy arcade interface for friends. Bright sky blue and icy blue backdrop, white rounded paper-like surfaces, bold cobalt blue primary controls, small coral orange and sunny yellow highlights, deep navy readable text. Exact palette direction: #EAF1FF backdrop, #FDFEFF surfaces, #2855F8 primary, #FF7750 coral, #FFD75E yellow, #182343 ink. Rounded heavy sans headings and friendly readable Vietnamese type. Chunky pill buttons with deliberate bottom-edge depth, subtle offset shadows, restrained dotted patterns and small confetti shapes. Polished cartoon character portraits with clay-like volume, expressive and friendly, richly colored but not noisy.
Desktop content: compact top brand with a white hat mascot icon, title "MŨ TRẮNG", room code and "Lời mời", "Luật chơi"; a round-progress track showing "Mô tả", "Thảo luận", "Bỏ phiếu", "Kết quả" with voting active; large heading "Bạn nghi ngờ ai?" and round label "Vòng 2"; a visible timer "43s". The main play area shows six neutral player portrait tiles with first names An, Bình, Chi, Dũng, Linh, Minh. One candidate is selected with a bold cobalt outline and a playful stamped check. Use selection and participation indicators, not role labels. Side action area has a closed private-word card reading "Từ riêng đang che" with "Xem từ", ballot summary "Chọn 1 người" and "Đã gửi 3/6", a prominent "Gửi phiếu" button, and a compact chat section. No secret word is shown.
Mobile: same title and active voting state; show portrait selection grid, compact timer and stage indicator, and a clear bottom "Gửi phiếu" action. Keep comfortable text sizes and large touch controls, minimal top clutter.
Gamification is grounded in real match state: progress track, participant portraits, selection stamps, countdown and tactile controls. No invented XP, points, coins, levels, leaderboard, rewards or changed game mechanics.
Constraints: visually replace a plain dark-green/gold card-dashboard interface entirely. No dark-green backgrounds, no gold casino palette, no generic SaaS dashboard, no analytics charts, no glassmorphism, no tables of data, no futuristic neon. Do not reveal which player is civilian, spy, or white hat. Avatars must not encode roles. Spell Vietnamese labels carefully. Make it feel like an engaging actual party game UI.
```
